The coral diamond denotes the first recorded specialist diagnosis of depression. Colored nodes represent subsequently recorded medical conditions; colors identify physiological systems. The integrated spatial display combines non-temporal comorbidity structure with temporal ordering of recorded diagnoses.

Temporal ordering refers to recorded diagnoses, not disease onset or causal progression. Networks for the sibling-matched cohort are constructed among individuals with depression and do not represent within-family estimates of condition–condition associations; familial confounding may remain.
Interactive results from a Swedish register-based study of psychiatric and somatic conditions following a first recorded specialist diagnosis of depression at ages 10–40 years.

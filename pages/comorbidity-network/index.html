Comorbidity Network Explorer

Nodes represent medical conditions significantly and positively associated with depression in the corresponding PheWAS. Edges represent significant positive adjusted associations between these conditions among individuals with depression.

Comorbidity odds ratios (ORs) were estimated using unconditional logistic regression, adjusted for the study covariates and other conditions associated with depression in the PheWAS. Community detection identified modules with greater within-module connectivity. Module labels are specific to each analysis and do not indicate identical composition across analyses.

Physiological Systems

Node size reflects condition frequency among individuals with depression, and node color indicates physiological system. Node sizes are scaled separately within each analysis and should not be compared across analyses. Edge thickness reflects the strength of the adjusted comorbidity association. Tooltips show uncapped adjusted ORs; the edge-filter scale is capped at 10. Network edges represent associations between conditions, not causal relationships or the order in which diagnoses were recorded.

The sibling-based PheWAS uses within-family comparisons. Its comorbidity networks are constructed among individuals with depression in the sibling-matched cohort. Condition–condition associations are not within-family estimates and may still be affected by familial confounding.
Interactive results from a Swedish register-based study of psychiatric and somatic conditions following a first recorded specialist diagnosis of depression at ages 10–40 years.

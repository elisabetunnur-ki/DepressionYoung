Physiological Systems

Arrows point from the earlier-recorded diagnosis to the later-recorded diagnosis. Node size reflects the number of individuals with depression who had the condition recorded and is scaled within the selected module; node color indicates physiological system. Node sizes should not be compared across modules or analyses. Edge tooltips report adjusted temporal-association ORs and counts for each recorded diagnostic order. The diagram combines pairwise temporal associations across the study cohort; it does not depict a single observed sequence shared by all individuals. Horizontal spacing is schematic and does not represent elapsed time.

Arrows summarize the observed ordering of recorded diagnoses and do not establish causal disease progression. A 6-month lag was applied after the index date. Temporal associations in the sibling-matched cohort were estimated among individuals with depression; they are not within-family estimates and may still be affected by familial confounding.
Interactive results from a Swedish register-based study of psychiatric and somatic conditions following a first recorded specialist diagnosis of depression at ages 10–40 years.

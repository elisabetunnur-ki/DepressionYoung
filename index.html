Explore phenome-wide associations, comorbidity networks, and the temporal ordering of recorded diagnoses following early-life depression, with an integrated 3D view. Results are available for population-based analyses overall and stratified by sex or age at depression diagnosis, and for sibling-matched analyses overall and by sex.

This nationwide Swedish register-based study examined subsequent psychiatric and somatic conditions after a first recorded specialist diagnosis of major depressive disorder at ages 10–40 years during 2001–2023. Subsequent diagnoses were ascertained from inpatient and specialist outpatient records in the Swedish Patient Register.
